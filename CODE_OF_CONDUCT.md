# Code of Conduct

Open Feedback Factory should be a useful place for serious builders to improve
agentic product infrastructure. Keep the collaboration direct, respectful, and
grounded in evidence.

## Expected Behavior

- Be clear, specific, and kind.
- Critique code, product choices, and safety tradeoffs without attacking people.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Assume good intent, but verify substance.
- Make room for maintainers to say no when a contribution weakens trust,
  security, or product coherence.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Sharing private data, credentials, raw feedback, or non-public repository
  details.
- Pressuring maintainers to bypass safety gates for speed or spectacle.
- Misrepresenting what the product can do.
- Using the project to spam users, maintainers, or external services.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove issues, PRs, comments, or access that
violate this code of conduct. Serious safety, privacy, or harassment concerns
should be raised privately with the maintainers.
